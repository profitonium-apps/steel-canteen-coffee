French press, four steps

  1. Grind coarse: about 2 tablespoons of coffee for every 6 oz of water.
  2. Heat the water to just off the boil, about 200°F.
  3. Pour, stir once and let it steep for 4 minutes.
  4. Press slowly, pour, and drink it while it is hot.
